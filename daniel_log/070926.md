Comments:
- Sometimes the robot has faulty logic despite seeing prepositions properly. Such cases were treated as a success, but the prompts may be optimized to handle these cases.
- Ex. ```"The carrot is sitting inside the plate area of the tray, not on top of it. Try placing the carrot on top of the tray instead!"```
- The most glaring failure is the near 0% success rate for "Behind" and "Under." Because the camera flattens a 3D scene into a 2D image, it lacks true depth perception.
- If a toy is placed behind the tray but remains fully visible, it simply appears higher up on the image's Y-axis. The AI interprets this as being "next to" or further away, rather than "behind."

## Next Steps: Prompt Tuning
Prioritize the 0% cases for spatial recognition (Under, Behind)

Based on these findings, your prompt engineering efforts should focus on two main fixes:
- Broaden Semantic Definitions: You need to explicitly tell the AI to treat slightly concave surfaces (like trays or shallow plates) as valid targets for the word "On."
- Redefine Depth Cues: Since the AI cannot rely on true depth sensors, you must prompt it to look for 2D contextual clues to determine "Behind" and "Under." For example, instructing the AI that if an object is partially occluded by the bottom edge of the reference item, it should evaluate it as "Behind."
